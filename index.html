11 days · self-drive · July

Montenegro

Coast to mountains and back. An 11-day self-drive loop through Kotor, Budva, Durmitor National Park, and the Tara canyon — some of the darkest, most beautiful water in Europe.

The days

One main thing a day, a sunset most evenings, apartments as a home base throughout.

01 Arrival

Landing in Kotor

Fly into Tivat (TIV), pick up the car at the airport, and drive ~10 km around the bay to Kotor. In the evening, take the cable car up the mountain ridge for sunset over the bay, then wander the old town after dark.

  • Cable car
  • Sunset
  • Old town at night
02 Coast

Bay of Kotor by boat → Budva

A four-hour boat tour of the bay covers the Blue Cave, a snorkel stop, the island churches, and the WWII submarine tunnel cut into the cliff. In the afternoon, drive ~25 km south to Budva. If the trip lines up with a Friday arrival, Chabad Budva hosts Friday-night services and dinner — book in advance.

  • Boat tour
  • Blue Cave
  • Bay of Kotor
  • Chabad Budva (Fri)
03 Coast

Budva: beach + old town

A slow day in Budva. Long shore walk into the old town and Mogren Beach at sunset. Chabad Budva runs Shabbat-morning services if the day falls on Shabbat.

  • Old town
  • Mogren Beach
  • Beach day
  • Chabad Budva (Shabbat)
04 Transit

Skadar Lake → up to the mountains

Morning kayak or paddle on Lake Skadar — lily pads, birdlife, total quiet. A short private cruise on the lake pairs well. In the afternoon, make the ~3-hour drive up into the mountains to Žabljak, the base for Durmitor National Park for the next four nights.

  • Kayaking
  • Lake cruise
  • Drive day
05 Mountains

Durmitor by jeep

A sunset jeep tour through Durmitor National Park — cliffs, canyons, high alpine lakes, fields of horses. The best introduction to the park.

  • Jeep tour
  • Durmitor NP
  • Sunset
06 Mountains

Rafting the Tara Canyon

A day rafting the Tara River through Europe’s deepest canyon. Afterwards, drive up to the Đurđevića Tara Bridge for sunset — fog rolling through the canyon is a regular show, the light shifting purple to gold.

  • Tara rafting
  • Đurđevića Tara Bridge
  • Sunset
07 Mountains

Black Lake hike

Forest hike out to Black Lake (Crno jezero) — a figure-eight glacial lake inside Durmitor NP, easy round-trip with frequent butterfly sightings. Swim if the weather holds; afternoon thunderstorms are common in the mountains and pass quickly.

  • Black Lake
  • Forest hike
  • Swim
08 Transit

Mountains → Kolašin

Morning at the Durmitor viewpoints and the Tara overlooks, then drive ~1 hr south through the mountains to Kolašin for an overnight in the pines — a useful stopover between the peaks and the coast.

  • Drive day
  • Kolašin
  • Forest stay
09 Transit

Back down to Budva

A ~2-hour drive from Kolašin back down to Budva on the coast for the final two nights of the loop. Second Friday on the coast, which makes this the second Chabad Budva Shabbat dinner if the dates align.

  • Drive day
  • Budva
  • Chabad Budva (Fri)
10 Coast

Coast day

A leisure day on the Adriatic — beach time, the old town, and the Sveti Stefan view down the coast. The water here is famously dark and clear. Second Shabbat-morning option at Chabad Budva if the day lands on Shabbat.

  • Sveti Stefan view
  • Beach
  • Old town
  • Chabad Budva (Shabbat)
11 Departure

Out from Tivat

Drop the car at Tivat and fly out — one last look at the bay from the plane window.

Getting around

Flights

Fly into Tivat (TIV) — the small coastal airport, right on the bay, an easy start and finish to the loop. Podgorica (TGD) is the alternative if Tivat fares don’t work, but adds a ~1.5 hr transfer.

The car

A self-drive loop. A small automatic (Toyota Yaris-class) is enough — pick it up at Tivat airport, drop it back there at the end. The mountain roads are narrow and winding but completely doable; the car is what makes this itinerary work.

Good to know

  • Use bank ATMs — airport and standalone ones charge painful fees.
  • Shops can close for multi-day holidays — stock the apartment early.
  • July is hot on the coast; the mountains are the cool escape.
  • Cash is useful in the mountains; cards are fine on the coast.
  • Chabad Budva is the kosher / Shabbat anchor for the coast — Friday-night dinner and Shabbat-morning services both weekends if the trip straddles them. Book ahead.
