The days

One thing a day, a sunset most nights, and a slow apartment to come home to.

01 Thu · Jul 11

Landing in Kotor

Flew Tel Aviv → Tivat, picked up the car at the airport, and drove around the bay to Kotor. In the evening we rode the cable car up the mountain for sunset — pomegranate juice at the top, the whole bay glowing below — then wandered the old town after dark.

  • Cable car
  • Sunset
  • Old town at night
02 Fri · Jul 12

Bay of Kotor by boat → Budva

A four-hour boat tour of the bay: the Blue Cave, a snorkel stop, the island churches, and the old WWII submarine tunnel cut into the cliff. Two dolphins swam alongside us. Then we drove down to Budva, checked in, and walked over to Chabad for Shabbat dinner.

  • Boat tour
  • Blue Cave
  • Dolphins
  • Erev Shabbat
03 Sat · Jul 13

Shabbat in Budva

A slow Shabbat on the coast. Services at the Budva Chabad, a long walk along the shore into the old town — an orchestra playing in the square — and Mogren Beach at sunset.

  • Shabbat
  • Old town
  • Mogren Beach
04 Sun · Jul 14

Skadar Lake → up to the mountains

We kayaked on Lake Skadar — lily pads, birds, total quiet — until our kayak quietly sank out from under us and we had to be fished out by a passing boat (everything in the dry bag survived; we laughed for an hour). We finished with a private cruise on the lake, then made the long drive up into the mountains to Žabljak, our home for the next four nights.

  • Kayaking
  • Lake cruise
  • The sinking-kayak story
05 Mon · Jul 15

Durmitor by jeep

Settled into the mountains and took a sunset jeep tour through Durmitor National Park — cliffs, canyons, high lakes, fields of horses and butterflies. Trance-like and gorgeous.

  • Jeep tour
  • Durmitor NP
  • Sunset
06 Tue · Jul 16

Rafting the Tara Canyon

Rafting the Tara River through Europe’s deepest canyon — a long route, just the two of us, ending in a thunderstorm. Afterwards we sat on the Tara Bridge for the best sunset of the whole trip: fog rolling through the canyon, the river vanishing and reappearing, the light shifting purple to gold.

  • Tara rafting
  • Đurđevića Tara Bridge
  • Best sunset
07 Wed · Jul 17 Birthday

Black Lake — birthday day

A forest walk out to Black Lake (Crno jezero) on Allison’s birthday. Blue butterflies, a figure-eight lake, then a sudden storm — thunder, rain, even hail — that turned the bright blue water dark and dramatic. We waited it out in a little hut, swam once the sun came back, and celebrated with Häagen-Dazs.

  • Black Lake
  • Storm + swim
  • Birthday ice cream
08 Thu · Jul 18

Mountains → Kolašin

Last morning around Durmitor and the Tara viewpoints, then a drive south through the mountains to Kolašin for a night at a little camp in the pines — a quiet stopover between the peaks and the coast.

  • Drive day
  • Kolašin
  • Camp in the pines
09 Fri · Jul 19

Back down to Budva

Drove back down to the coast and into Budva for our last two nights. Checked into the apartment, then over to Chabad again for our second Shabbat.

Last Shabbat by the sea

A second, easy Shabbat in Budva — the beach, the old town, the Sveti Stefan view down the coast. The Adriatic here is famously dark and clear; we just let the day be slow.

Getting around

Flights

EL AL, Tel Aviv ↔ Tivat (TIV), booked with Matmid points. Out Thursday July 11, home Sunday July 21. Tivat is the small coastal airport — right on the bay, an easy start and finish.

The car

We rented a Toyota Yaris (automatic) and picked it up at Tivat airport, then drove the whole loop ourselves — coast, mountains, and back. The mountain roads are narrow and winding but completely doable; by the end it felt easy. A car is what makes this itinerary work.

Good to know

  • Use bank ATMs — the airport/standalone ones charged painful fees.
  • Shops can close for multi-day holidays — stock the apartment early.
  • It’s hot in July on the coast; the mountains are the cool escape.
  • Chabad in Budva was our Shabbat home both weekends.
